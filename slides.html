Problem Statement & Motivation Slide 2 / 10
The Image Spam Threat

Spammers bypass text-based spam filters (keyword scanners, NLP, TF-IDF) by embedding fraudulent messages inside image pixels.

  • Promotional banners, fake jackpots, phishing vouchers.
  • Traditional mail filters cannot read raw pixel data.
  • OCR is computationally heavy and easily fooled by font distortions and background patterns.
The Computer Vision Solution

Exploiting fundamental differences in statistical visual distributions between spam graphics and natural images:

  • Color Spaces: High-saturation synthetic primary colors vs. natural landscape & indoor palettes.
  • Gradient Sharpness: Dense text outlines, border frames, and geometric banners.
  • Bayesian Probabilistic Model: Fast, interpretable, and lightweight.
Feature Engineering: Class Histograms Slide 3 / 10
3D RGB Histogram
16 × 16 × 16 = 4,096 Bins

Quantizes 24-bit RGB space. Captures fine color distribution differences between synthetic banners and natural scenes.

2D Hue-Saturation (HSV)
18 H × 16 S = 288 Bins

Decouples chromaticity from illumination intensity. Isolates garish promotional colors (neon red, yellow) irrespective of brightness.

1D Sobel Edge Magnitude
16 Gradient Bins

Calculated as √(Ix2 + Iy2). Captures synthetic text density, exclamation marks, and sharp banner borders.

Mathematical Formulation: Bayes' Decision Rule Slide 4 / 10
P(Spam | Image) = [ P(Image | Spam) · P(Spam) ] / P(Image)
Laplace (Additive) Smoothing

Unseen colors in test images produce zero counts ($P=0$). Laplace smoothing eliminates zero-frequency traps:

P(bin | C) = [ Count(bin) + α ] / [ Σ Count + α·K ]
Log-Odds Posterior Formulation

Summing log-likelihood ratios avoids numerical underflow:

Log-Odds = ln(P(Spam)/P(Ham)) + γ · (Δcolor + βΔedge)
P(Spam | Image) = σ(Log-Odds)
Spatial Explainability: Likelihood Heatmaps Slide 5 / 10

Unlike black-box neural networks, our Bayesian classifier provides complete pixel-level transparency:

1. Pixel Prior-Likelihood

Computes P(Spam | pixel) for each (x, y) coordinate based on 3D RGB class histograms.

2. JET Colormapping

Scales posterior into [0, 255]. Red = High spam evidence; Blue = Clean natural tone.

3. Alpha-Blended Overlay

0.55 × Original + 0.45 × Heatmap overlays suspicious regions directly on the image.

System Architecture & Pipeline Slide 6 / 10
Training Pipeline (Offline)
  1. 100 Training Images (50 Spam, 50 Ham).
  2. Accumulate 3D RGB, 2D HS, and Edge histograms.
  3. Apply Laplace Additive Smoothing (α = 1.0).
  4. Serialize class distributions to bayesian_model.npz.
Inference Pipeline (Online Web)
  1. Input image uploaded via Flask REST API.
  2. Extract feature histograms in OpenCV.
  3. Evaluate Bayesian log-odds & sigmoid posterior.
  4. Generate Likelihood Heatmap & 4-panel diagnostic.
  5. Inference completed in < 20 milliseconds!
