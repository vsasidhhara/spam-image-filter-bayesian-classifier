Key Insights:

  • Edge Gradient Magnitude: Spam images exhibit prominent probability mass at higher gradient bins due to crisp text typography, sharp rectangular coupon badges, and exclamation marks. Natural ham images exhibit predominantly smooth transitions.
  • Hue Chromaticity: Spam distributions concentrate around attention-grabbing banner hues (saturated red, yellow, high-contrast cyan), while ham distributions center around landscape greens, sky blues, and organic neutrals.
  • Color Saturation: Spam features a sharp peak near maximum saturation ($S > 180$), whereas natural photos have rich gradations of pastel and muted mid-level saturations.
